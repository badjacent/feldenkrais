# Interior Landscape
## Finding the hidden surfaces of bone

---

Notice where your collarbones rest against the floor through your back.

...

The weight of your shoulders. How they settle into their place.

...

See if you can find your left collarbone with your attention. Not with your hand. Just your attention.

...

The front surface of that bone. The part that faces the ceiling.

...

Interesting to notice — your attention probably landed on the outside. The surface closest to the world.

...

What if you could slip your attention underneath? To the surface that faces inward. The underside of your left collarbone.

...

Starting near your breastbone. The underside of the bone there.

...

Let your attention travel slowly along that hidden surface. From the center of your chest toward your left shoulder.

...

The texture of attention changes as you move along the underside. Some places feel more available than others.

...

Now drop your attention to a rib on the left side. Any rib. Notice which one called to you.

...

Again, you're probably on the outside surface. The part that would touch a shirt.

...

See if you can find the inside surface of that same rib. The surface that faces your lung.

...

With your eyes closed, let your eyes look toward that rib. From the inside.

...

Your eyes behind closed lids, looking at the inner surface of your own rib.

...

Now imagine a hand resting on the outside of that same rib. A gentle hand. While your eyes stay on the inside.

...

Imagine a line between your eyes and that hand. Through the bone itself.

...

The imagined hand gently grasps the rib. Draws it outward just a hair's width. Then releases.

...

Let the space breathe.

...

Now reverse it. The imagined hand moves to the inside of the rib. Your closed eyes shift to look from the outside.

...

The hand on the inside draws the rib inward. Just barely. Then releases.

...

Let the space breathe.

...

See if you can create a line between your left collarbone and that rib. Not a real line. A relationship. From the inside surface of one to the inside surface of the other.

...

The line softens. Dissolves. Just the memory of where your attention has been.

...

Notice the right side of your chest now. You haven't done anything there. Just notice the difference.

...

There's nothing to fix. Just something to notice.

...

Find your right collarbone with your attention. The underside, near your breastbone.

...

Travel along that hidden surface toward your right shoulder.

...

Drop to a rib on the right. The inside surface.

...

Eyes looking from within. Hand touching from without. The line between them through bone.

...

Hand draws outward. Releases. The space breathes.

...

Hand inside, eyes outside. Draw inward. Release. The space breathes.

...

A line between right collarbone and right rib. Interior to interior. Then let it fade.

...

Both sides visited now. No lines to maintain. Nothing to hold.

...

The weight of your bones against the floor. Some bones have an inside now. Some always did.

...

Rest.
