# QuietBones — Elevator Speech

**QuietBones** is a relaxation app that teaches you to relax from the inside.

Most relaxation apps tell you to relax your whole body at once — like turning down a dimmer switch. QuietBones is different. Each exploration guides your attention to a specific bone, a specific surface, a specific relationship — and something lets go there, just there. Over time, you develop an intuition for how to release tension locally, precisely, whenever you need to.

The explorations are simple and quiet. You lie down, close your eyes, and follow your attention through your skeleton — micromovements, imagined lines, shifting between the inside and outside of a bone. No mantras. No breathing exercises. No spiritual framework. Just your bones, your attention, and the space between them.

It's not meditation, exactly. It's not exercise. It's more like learning to listen to the architecture of your own body.

**For the Calm/Headspace audience** who wants something more embodied and less conceptual. **For people with chronic tension** who've tried everything and need a new way in. **For anyone who's curious** about what it feels like to feel a bone from the inside.
