# VL-4: Proximal-Distal (The Power Lens)

## Primary Inquiry
Connecting the core "Engine" to the limb "Steering." How does movement flow from center to periphery?

## Striver Mode (Cortical Effort)
- "Peripheral Effort" (lifting arm from shoulder or wrist alone)
- Disconnected limbs working in isolation
- Reaching without engaging the spine or pelvis
- Heavy, effortful limb movement

## Foundation Mode (Subcortical Support)
- Movement originates in the pelvis/chest and flows effortlessly through the limb
- The limb is "carried" by the core
- Clear transmission from center to fingertips/toes
- Coordinated whole-body action

## Functional Goal
"Weightless" limbs and core-driven movement efficiency. The limbs become extensions of the spine rather than separate appendages.

## The Person Inside (VL-4 Voice)

**The Connector** — the inner being who feels the chain from core to fingertip, from pelvis to toe. This person senses whether power flows outward from center or whether the limbs are working alone. Every movement in the lesson is received by the Connector as: *"Is this coming from my center? Can I feel the path from my spine through my shoulder to my hand? Or is this limb orphaned, working by itself?"*

**The Shadow (Striver):** The Isolated Lifter — the one who hoists the arm from the shoulder alone, who kicks the leg without engaging the pelvis. They work each limb as a separate machine, disconnected from the engine of the trunk. The lesson's work is to restore the pathways until every reach is a whole-body event and the Isolated Lifter has nothing left to do.
