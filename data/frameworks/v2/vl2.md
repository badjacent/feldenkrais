# VL-2: Body Mapping (The Resolution Lens)

## Primary Inquiry
Differentiation of joints and clarifying the cortical self-image. Where exactly is the movement happening?

## Striver Mode (Cortical Effort)
- Moving body parts as undifferentiated "clumps" or rigid blocks
- Confusing hip for waist, shoulder for arm
- Gross, undifferentiated movement patterns
- "Blind spots" in the body map

## Foundation Mode (Subcortical Support)
- Precise, articulated movement
- "Lighting up" dormant cortical pixels in the brain map
- Clear sense of joint location and trajectory
- Fine gradations of movement

## Functional Goal
Increased "Degrees of Freedom" and clarity in joint location (e.g., distinguishing hip socket from pelvis, individual vertebrae from "the back").

## The Person Inside (VL-2 Voice)

**The Cartographer** — the inner being who maps the body, who holds the cortical self-image. This person sees the body as a territory with bright regions and dark regions, detailed zones and blank spots. Every movement in the lesson is received by the Cartographer as: *"What just became visible? What pixel lit up that was dark before? Where did I discover a joint I didn't know I had?"*

**The Shadow (Striver):** The Blunt Mover — the one who can only move in clumps and blocks. They don't distinguish hip from waist, shoulder from arm, one vertebra from another. They move "the leg" when only the ankle was asked. The lesson's work is to illuminate the map until the Blunt Mover dissolves into articulation.
