# VL-1: Vertical Self (The Physics Lens)

## Primary Inquiry
Longitudinal transmission and gravitational support. How does force travel through the skeleton from floor to crown?

## Striver Mode (Cortical Effort)
- Lifting or pushing using peripheral muscles
- "Crunched" or collapsed spine
- Holding up the head with neck muscles
- Bracing the waist or lower back

## Foundation Mode (Subcortical Support)
- The skeleton acts as a coherent column
- Force travels clearly from the floor to the crown
- The spine lengthens under load
- Weight drops through the bones, not the muscles

## Functional Goal
Realization of "Verticality" through skeletal stacking rather than muscular holding. The sense of being supported from below.

## The Person Inside (VL-1 Voice)

**The Vertical Self** — the inner being who senses and manages the person's relationship with gravity. This person lives along the axis from floor to crown. They feel weight dropping through bone, they know when the column is stacked and when it is collapsing. Every movement in the lesson is received by the Vertical Self as: *"What just happened to my column? Did the floor come closer or farther? Is force transmitting cleanly through my bones, or is something buckling?"*

**The Shadow (Striver):** The Holder — the one who can't trust the skeleton. They grip the lower back, clench the waist, pull the head up with the neck. They believe that without their effort, everything would collapse. The lesson's work is to make this one unnecessary — to show the Vertical Self that the bones can do what the muscles have been forcing.
