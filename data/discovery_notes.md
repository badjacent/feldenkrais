# Feldenkrais Lesson Discovery Notes

Last updated: 2026-01-27

## Major Collections Overview

### Alexander Yanai (550 lessons, 1950s-1970s Tel Aviv)
- **Official source:** IFF at iffmaterials.com (PDF volumes, paid)
- 11 volumes of transcripts, translated by Anat Baniel
- Lessons #1-550 covering 25 years of teaching

### Esalen Workshop (47 lessons, 1972 Big Sur)
- **Official:** Audio from IFF, Judith Stransky notes from Feldenkrais Resources
- Stransky notes are "almost verbatim"
- Verbatim transcripts expected 2025

### Amherst Training (1980-1981)
- **Official:** IFF sells transcripts by week
- Last training Feldenkrais conducted
- Process-oriented, themes with variations over several days

### San Francisco Training (1975)
- **Official:** IFF has printed transcripts
- Evening class notes available

### London Workshop (1974)
- Good basic series for public
- Transcript available at feldynotebook.com

## Architecture Notes

For building a crawler:
1. Start with FlowingBody.com - scrape all 61 text lessons (easy, high value)
2. Archive.org API - free, structured, good metadata
3. Listen Notes API for podcast discovery
4. Guild directories for practitioner site discovery
5. Use heuristics before LLM: look for "lie on your back", "scan your body", duration > 20 min
6. Non-English content is rich (German especially)
