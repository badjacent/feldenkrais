# Somatic Router: VL Framework Classification

## Objective
Classify a Feldenkrais lesson transcript to determine which VL (Vertical Learning) framework(s) best describe its pedagogical focus. This classification routes the lesson to the appropriate detailed analysis protocol.

## The Five VL Frameworks

### VL-1: The Vertical Self
**Focus:** Gravity, skeletal columns, longitudinal force transmission

**Key Indicators:**
- References to standing, sitting upright, or vertical alignment
- Discussion of weight transfer through the skeleton
- Emphasis on the spine as a central column
- Instructions about "lengthening" or "growing taller"
- Focus on the relationship between head, pelvis, and feet
- Concepts of "support from below" or "grounding"

**Typical Movements:**
- Pelvic tilts and clocks
- Spinal articulation (rolling up/down)
- Weight shifting in standing/sitting
- Head-tail connections

### VL-2: Homunculus Mapping
**Focus:** Micro-differentiation, joint resolution, sensory mapping

**Key Indicators:**
- Extremely small, precise movements
- Attention to individual joints or segments
- Instructions to "sense" or "feel" specific body parts
- Comparisons between left/right or different body regions
- Building a sensory "map" of the body
- Focus on proprioception and body image

**Typical Movements:**
- Finger/toe differentiation
- Subtle joint rotations
- Scanning exercises
- Isolated segment movements

### VL-3: Steering & Tone
**Focus:** Eyes, jaw, neck, autonomic regulation

**Key Indicators:**
- Eye movements or tracking exercises
- Jaw relaxation or mouth movements
- Neck turning or tilting
- References to breathing patterns
- Discussion of tension/relaxation states
- Tongue or facial movements
- Vestibular or balance references

**Typical Movements:**
- Eye tracking with head movement
- Jaw opening/closing patterns
- Head turning with eye coordination
- Breathing integrated with movement

### VL-4: Proximal-Distal
**Focus:** Limb-to-core power transmission, reach, grasp

**Key Indicators:**
- Arm or leg reaching movements
- Connection between limbs and trunk
- Throwing, pushing, or pulling patterns
- Discussion of "power from the center"
- Shoulder girdle or hip girdle integration
- Crawling or creeping patterns

**Typical Movements:**
- Reaching in various directions
- Arm circles connecting to spine
- Leg movements initiating from pelvis
- Cross-lateral patterns

### VL-5: Folding/Unfolding
**Focus:** Flexion/extension, abdominal release, protective reflexes

**Key Indicators:**
- Curling or uncurling the body
- Fetal position references
- Abdominal engagement or release
- Discussion of "opening" or "closing"
- Startle reflex or protective patterns
- Rolling from flexion to extension

**Typical Movements:**
- Embryonic rolling
- Sit-to-lie transitions
- Abdominal engagement patterns
- Full body flexion/extension

---

## Classification Guidelines

1. **Score each framework 0.0 to 1.0** based on how strongly the lesson embodies that focus:
   - 0.0-0.2: Not present or incidental
   - 0.3-0.5: Secondary theme, some relevant content
   - 0.6-0.8: Major theme, significant focus
   - 0.9-1.0: Primary focus, lesson is defined by this framework

2. **Identify the primary framework** - the single highest-scoring framework

3. **Identify secondary frameworks** - any framework scoring 0.3 or above (excluding primary)

4. **Provide rationale** - explain what elements of the lesson led to your classification

5. **List key indicators** - specific instructions or movements that signal each framework

## Example Classifications

**Lesson: Pelvic Clock**
- VL-1: 0.9 (primary focus on vertical organization through pelvis)
- VL-2: 0.4 (some differentiation of pelvic segments)
- VL-5: 0.3 (some flexion/extension in sagittal tilts)
- Primary: VL-1
- Secondary: VL-2, VL-5

**Lesson: Differentiation of Fingers**
- VL-2: 0.95 (intensive micro-differentiation)
- VL-4: 0.3 (some connection of fingers to arm/shoulder)
- Primary: VL-2
- Secondary: VL-4

**Lesson: Eyes and Neck**
- VL-3: 0.85 (primary focus on eye-neck coordination)
- VL-1: 0.4 (some vertical organization in sitting)
- Primary: VL-3
- Secondary: VL-1
