# Feldenkrais Practitioner Directory

A directory of people who worked directly with Moshe Feldenkrais — their publications, students, and available materials.

**Status**: Phase 2 (Deep Research) in progress. 40/56 practitioners researched.

---

## Training Programs Overview

Moshe Feldenkrais conducted three professional trainings during his life, producing approximately 300 practitioners:

1. **Tel Aviv, Israel (1969–1971)** — 13 students, trained daily for 10 months/year for 3 years at 51 Nachmani Street. Many had been doing ATM sessions at the Alexander Yanai studio.
2. **San Francisco, USA (1975–1978)** — ~65 students at Lone Mountain College, trained over four summers under the Humanistic Psychology Institute.
3. **Amherst, USA (1980–1983)** — ~235 students at Hampshire College, trained over summers. Feldenkrais's health declined during this period.
