# Bell Hand Lesson

[Initial Tilting and Mapping]
  1. Scan the body while lying on the back; then transition to lying on the left side with the right foot standing behind the left leg.
  2. Tilt the right knee inside and outside at various foot distances to explore range and limitation.
  3. Return to the back and rest; scan for initial changes.

[The Tibia Seesaw]
  4. Lying on the left side, rest the right tibia on the left leg to find a fulcrum; perform a seesaw motion (knee toward floor, then foot toward floor).
  5. Rest on the back and scan for differences in the hip joints and pelvic floor.

[Integrating Seesaw with Bell Hand]
  6. Return to the left side and repeat the original knee tilting movement, varying the speed and foot position.
  7. Place the right tibia on the left leg; initiate the seesaw motion again.
  8. Place the right hand on the stomach; feel the breath/belly movement and begin a circular caressing motion (bell hand).
  9. Combine the circular stomach caressing with the lower body seesaw; notice the movement of the right elbow.
  10. Rest on the back and integrate.

[Refining Lightness and Elbow Circles]
  11. Return to the side; revisit the knee tilting (knee to floor) with variable speeds and 'silly' movements.
  12. Add pulsing/flexing of the fingers to the circular hand motion while maintaining the seesaw; focus on the lightness of the elbow.
  13. Rest on the back and scan for internal changes.

[Sliding and Pendulums]
  14. Lying on left side, circle the hand/elbow while sliding the right foot forward and backward on the floor.
  15. Lift the right foot slightly and use the knee as a pendulum while continuing the belly/bell hand circles.
  16. Rest on the back; observe the sensation of sinking into the floor.

[Forward-Backward Hand/Knee Slides]
  17. On the left side, place right knee on the floor behind the left; stand the right palm on the floor in front; slide the palm forward and back.
  18. Move the right hand and right knee forward and backward together on the floor.
  19. Brief rest on the back.

[Oppositional Circles (Coupe de Gras)]
  20. On the left side, move the hand and knee together in up/down and circular paths.
  21. Perform the 'coupe de gras': move the hand in one circular direction and the knee in the opposite circular direction.
  22. Final rest on back; transition to standing and walking.
