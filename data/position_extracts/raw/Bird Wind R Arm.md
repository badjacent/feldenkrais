# Bird Wind R Arm

[Initial Mapping and Head Tilting]
  1. Observe the contact of the shoulder blades and ribs on the floor; trace the outline of the shoulder blades mentally.
  2. Roll the head left and right; then tilt the head (ear to shoulder) while keeping the nose to the ceiling.

[Wrist Rotation and Lifting]
  3. Rotate the right hand and wrist, turning the palm down and then further until the back of the hand touches the thigh.
  4. Lift the right wrist while keeping fingertips in contact with the floor; then combine with the internal rotation toward the thigh.
  5. Rest on the back.

[Spinning the Hand on Floor and Chest]
  6. Sitting: Place the back of the right hand on the floor and explore spinning the arm by moving the elbow and shoulder.
  7. Rest on the back.
  8. Place the back of the right hand on the chest; rotate the hand so fingertips point up to the face and then down to the feet.
  9. Sitting: Repeat the rotation of the back of the right hand on the chest/stomach, moving the elbow up and down like a wing.
  10. Rest on the back and integrate.

[The Hand in the Armpit: Wing Folding]
  11. Lying: Slide the back of the right hand from the chest into the right armpit; then gently lift and lower the elbow.
  12. Rest and observe differences between the two sides.

[Side Bending and Rib Pressing]
  13. Standing: Slide the left hand down the side to the knee; then add the right hand in the armpit, lifting the right elbow.
  14. Standing: Use the left hand over the head to the right ear; bend left, and use the right hand to press into the ribs to return to center.
  15. Lying: Repeat the side bending with the right hand in the armpit and left hand over the head to the right ear.

[Elbow Circles with Pelvic and Spinal Integration]
  16. Lying: With the right hand in the armpit, use the left hand to guide the right elbow in circles in both directions.
  17. Lying: Continue elbow circles with the right foot standing, using pelvic rotation to facilitate the movement.
  18. Standing: Circle the right elbow with the hand in the armpit, letting the eyes, chest, and torso follow the movement.
  19. Standing: Deepen the elbow circles, bending the whole self down toward the floor and up toward the ceiling.
  20. Sitting: Right hand in armpit; move the elbow forward to round the back and backward to open the chest.
  21. Sitting: Combine rounding/extending the spine with large elbow circles guided by the left hand.

[Bilateral Wing Flapping and Integration]
  22. Lying: Return to 'flopping the wing' with the right hand in the armpit; test the lifting of both arms separately.
  23. Lying: Backs of both hands on lower ribs; lift and lower elbows together and alternately like two wings.
  24. Lying: Slide both hands into armpits while pulling elbows down; then lift elbows toward the ceiling to 'flap' quickly.
  25. Final scan: Observe the resting contact of the back, shoulders, and pelvis.
