# Baby Rolls with Flexion

[Lying on back, legs long]
- Scan body contact and the arch of the low back
- Roll the head gently side to side

[Lying on right side, knees bent, right arm under head, left hand on floor in front]
- Push the middle of the back toward the wall behind you and return
- Lead with the left shoulder to twist the spine slightly backward and return
- Push the back toward the wall while bringing the chin toward the chest
- Place the left hand behind the head and pull the chin to the chest while pushing the back toward the wall
- Rest on the side or back

[Lying on right side, knees bent]
- Push the back toward the wall while inhaling
- Push the back toward the wall while exhaling
- Hold the left knee with the left hand and bring the knee toward the chest while pushing the back toward the wall
- Hold the left knee with the left hand and the head with the right hand, bringing the head and knee toward each other
- Rest on the back

[Lying on right side, knees bent]
- Lift the left leg away from the right leg, moving it back and toward the chest to find lightness
- Rest on the back
- Hold the left knee with the left hand, bring the forehead toward the knee, and roll toward the back
- Lift the knee and round the back to roll completely from the side onto the back
- Rest on the back

[Lying on back, knees toward chest]
- Roll back and forth between the right side and the back while curled in a ball
- Bring forearms together in front of the face, then lift the left leg and arm to roll onto the back
- Roll from side to side like a ball, softening the chest and belly
- Roll side to side while looking at the hands
- Roll side to side while looking toward the feet
- Rest on the back

[Lying on back, knees held over chest, arms held in front]
- Roll side to side like a pill bug
- Rest with legs long and scan body contact
- Roll the head side to side
- Roll to the side to sit up, stand, and walk
