# Bird Wind R Arm

[Lying on back, legs long]
- Sense the contact of the shoulder blades and ribs on the floor
- Roll the head left and right
- Tilt the head bringing ear toward shoulder (nose stays to ceiling)
- Rotate the right hand until the back of the hand touches the thigh
- Lift the right wrist while keeping fingertips touching the floor
- Rotate the right hand toward the thigh while lifting the wrist
- Rest

[Sitting]
- Place the back of the right hand on the floor
- Spin the arm by moving the right elbow and shoulder
- Rest on the back

[Sitting]
- Place the back of the right hand on the chest or stomach
- Rotate the hand so fingertips point up to the face, then down to the feet
- Move the right elbow up and down like a wing
- Rest on the back

[Lying on back]
- Slide the back of the right hand into the right armpit
- Lift and lower the right elbow
- Rest

[Standing]
- Slide the left hand down the left leg toward the knee
- Place the right hand in the right armpit and lift the right elbow while sliding left
- Reach the left hand over the head to the right ear and bend to the left
- Use the right hand to press into the ribs to return to center

[Lying on back]
- Place the right hand in the right armpit and the left hand over the head to the right ear
- Bend the torso to the side
- Use the left hand to guide the right elbow in circles
[Lying on back, right foot standing]
- Circle the right elbow using the movement of the pelvis and hips

[Standing]
- Place the right hand in the right armpit
- Circle the right elbow while the eyes, chest, and torso follow
- Make large elbow circles while bending the whole body down toward the floor and up to the ceiling

[Sitting]
- Place the right hand in the right armpit
- Move the right elbow forward to round the back
- Move the right elbow backward to arch the chest
- Use the left hand to guide the right elbow in large circles while rounding and arching the spine

[Lying on back]
- Place the right hand in the right armpit and lift/lower the elbow
- Place the backs of both hands on the lower ribs
- Lift and lower both elbows together, then alternately
- Slide both hands into the armpits
- Pull both elbows down toward the waist, then lift them toward the ceiling in a fast flapping motion
- Rest and observe the contact of the back, shoulders, and pelvis
