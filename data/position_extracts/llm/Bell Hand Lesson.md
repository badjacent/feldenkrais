# Bell Hand Lesson

[Lying on back]
- Scan the body

[Lying on left side, right foot standing on the floor behind the left leg]
- Tilt the right knee toward the floor and back
- Tilt the right knee at various distances from the body
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest and scan

[Lying on left side, right shin resting across the left leg]
- Seesaw the right leg (tilt knee toward floor, then foot toward floor)
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest and scan the hips and pelvis

[Lying on left side, right foot standing behind the left leg]
- Tilt the right knee toward the floor at different speeds

[Lying on left side, right shin resting across the left leg]
- Seesaw the right leg (knee toward floor, then foot toward floor)
- Place right hand on the stomach and make circular caressing motions
- Combine the leg seesaw with the circular hand motions
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest

[Lying on left side, right foot standing behind the left leg]
- Tilt the right knee toward the floor with "silly" or variable movements

[Lying on left side, right shin resting across the left leg]
- Combine the leg seesaw with circular hand motions on the stomach
- Add pulsing or flexing of the fingers to the hand circles
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest and scan

[Lying on left side]
- Circle the right hand and elbow while sliding the right foot forward and back on the floor
- Lift the right foot and swing the knee like a pendulum while circling the hand on the stomach
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest and notice the body sinking into the floor

[Lying on left side, right knee on floor behind the left, right palm on floor in front]
- Slide the right palm forward and back on the floor
- Slide the right hand and right knee forward and back together
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Rest

[Lying on left side]
- Move the right hand and right knee together in circles
- Move the right hand in a circle while moving the right knee in the opposite circle
- Transition to lying on back

[Lying on back]
- Final rest and scan
- Transition to standing

[Standing and walking]
- Notice changes in balance and movement
