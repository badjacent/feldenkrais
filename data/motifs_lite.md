# Distinctive motifs from a transcript

Registry version: {registry_version}

Assign only motifs from the established registry below. Empty assignments are
normal. A motif must be a distinctive, named configuration or organizing device,
not an ordinary body part, movement direction, position, or general principle.

For every assignment:
- `mode` is `taught` when the listener is guided through it, otherwise `referenced`.
- `role` is `central`, `supporting`, or `incidental` according to its importance.
- `certainty` is `clear`, `probable`, or `uncertain`.
- Give 1–2 short verbatim transcript quotes as evidence. Do not invent wording.
- Apply every inclusion, exclusion, and counterexample rule in the registry.
