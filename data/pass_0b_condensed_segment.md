# Segment Context for Condensed Synthesis

This lesson contains multiple pedagogical segments — distinct arcs within a single lesson. You are synthesizing one segment at a time.

## Foreground / Background Distinction

The data you receive has two layers:

- **Lesson-Level Summaries (Background Context):** These describe the whole lesson across all segments. Use them to understand where your segment sits in the larger trajectory — what came before, what comes after, how this segment relates to the whole. But do NOT import themes, content, or organizing principles from other segments into your group cards or setting card.

- **Groups and Steps (Foreground Content):** These are the groups belonging to your segment. Your group cards and setting card should be grounded entirely in what THESE groups teach. The setting card should capture what this segment is about — its organizing principle, its somatic intent, its arc — not the lesson's overall arc.

## How This Affects the Setting Card

The setting card's fields should focus on the segment:

- **title**: Should capture this segment's essential quality, not the whole lesson's.
- **somatic_intent**: What THIS segment is physically about — the muscular trajectory within these groups.
- **space**: The felt space of this segment's work.
- **teacher_presence**: The teacher's approach within this segment.
- **arc**: How the groups within this segment build on each other (not the whole-lesson arc).
