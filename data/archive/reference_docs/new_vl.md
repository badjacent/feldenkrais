# The Council of Five: VL-1 to VL-5

This framework deconstructs the Feldenkrais experience into five core systems of intelligence. Each has a functional domain (Proper Name) and an agentic persona (The Person-Inside).

---

### **The Negotiation States (The "Washing" Process)**
* **Bracing:** The Striver (cortical ego) is in control; high noise, tension, and muscular effort.
* **Softening:** The Washing is in progress; confusion, exploration, and the melting of habit.
* **Transmission:** The Foundation (subcortical self) has taken over; skeletal clarity and "involuntary" ease.

---

### **VL-1: The Riser**
* **The Person:** **The Riser**
* **The Domain:** Gravity, skeletal columns, and longitudinal force.
* **The Drive:** **Integrity.**
* **Perspective:** "I am the tension in the web that creates height. I carry the pressure wave from the earth to the crown. If the columns are stacked, I am silent and strong."

### **VL-2: Cartographer**
* **The Person:** **Cartographer**
* **The Domain:** Micro-differentiation and joint resolution.
* **The Drive:** **Illumination toward clarity**
* **Perspective:** "I am the map and the resolution of the map. I am obsessed with the pixels of sensation. I notice the exact millimeter of 'slush' in the joints and turn the 'blob' into a high-definition image."

### **VL-3: Steering / Tone**
* **The Person:** **The Tuner**
* **The Domain:** Eyes, jaw, neck, and autonomic regulation.
* **The Drive:** **Congruency.**
* **Perspective:** "I match the internal tone to the external horizon. I lead through the eyes. If I am quiet and aligned, the council can work. If I am tight, the world feels smaller."

### **VL-4: Core and Reach**
* **The Person:** **Proximal-Distal**
* **The Domain:** Limb-to-core power and skeletal leverage.
* **The Drive:** **Potency.**
* **Perspective:** "I am the bridge between the powerhouse and the instrument. My center supports so my edge can be free. I ensure the hand is weightless because the spine is doing the work."

### **VL-5: Frontal Line Guardian**
* **The Person:** **Flexor-Extensor**
* **The Domain:** Flexion/extension and the protective/opening reflexes.
* **The Drive:** **Permission.**
* **Perspective:** "I manage the 'soft' front of the self. I know how to fold for protection and how to unfurl for action. I decide when the Vertical Self is allowed to stand tall."
