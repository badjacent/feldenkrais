# Master Logic Document: VL-Framework Synthesis (Pass B)

## 1. Core Evaluation Logic
For every "Somatic Event" identified in Pass A, the Synthesis Agent must evaluate the **Negotiation State** by applying the following heuristic:

* **Bracing:** The Shadow is in control; high noise, tension, and parasitic effort.
    * *Indicators:* Localized effort, speed, gripping, holding breath, linear "trying."
* **Softening:** The Washing is in progress; confusion, exploration, and the melting of habit.
    * *Indicators:* "Washing" markers (oscillation, rhythm), micro-movements, healthy confusion, trial and error.
* **Transmission:** The Council has taken over; skeletal clarity and "involuntary" ease.
    * *Indicators:* "Ease," "lightness," global involvement, skeletal transmission, "involuntary" quality.

## 2. Framework-Specific Logic Modules

### [VL-1] The Riser (The Physics Lens)
* **Primary Inquiry:** Longitudinal transmission and gravitational support.
* **Shadow Mode:** Lifting or pushing using peripheral muscles; "crunched" or collapsed spine.
* **Foundation Mode:** The skeleton acts as a coherent column; force travels clearly from the floor to the crown.
* **Functional Goal:** Realization of "Verticality" through skeletal stacking rather than muscular holding.


### [VL-2] Homunculus Mapping (The Resolution Lens)
* **Primary Inquiry:** Differentiation of joints and clarifying the cortical self-image.
* **Shadow Mode:** Moving body parts as undifferentiated "clumps" or rigid blocks.
* **Foundation Mode:** Precise, articulated movement; "lighting up" dormant cortical pixels in the brain map.
* **Functional Goal:** Increased "Degrees of Freedom" and clarity in joint location (e.g., hip socket vs. pelvis).


### [VL-3] Steering & Tone (The Regulatory Lens)
* **Primary Inquiry:** Inhibiting reflexive tension via the eyes, jaw, and tongue.
* **Shadow Mode:** "Hard eyes" (staring); jaw clenching; bracing of the neck and upper traps.
* **Foundation Mode:** "Soft eyes"; liquid neck; decoupling of sensory organs from reflexive motor habits.
* **Functional Goal:** Autonomic down-regulation and sub-occipital release.


### [VL-4] Proximal-Distal (The Power Lens)
* **Primary Inquiry:** Connecting the core "Engine" to the limb "Steering."
* **Shadow Mode:** "Peripheral Effort" (e.g., trying to lift an arm from the shoulder or wrist alone).
* **Foundation Mode:** Movement originates in the pelvis/chest and flows effortlessly through the limb.
* **Functional Goal:** "Weightless" limbs and core-driven movement efficiency.


### [VL-5] Folding-Unfolding (The Geometric Lens)
* **Primary Inquiry:** Navigating the protective flexion reflex vs. the active extension.
* **Shadow Mode:** "Red Light Reflex" (habitual abdominal bracing); muscular resistance to opening the front.
* **Foundation Mode:** Ability to round and arch the spine with equal neutrality and no residual tension.
* **Functional Goal:** Elimination of "parasitic" abdominal or back bracing; spine neutrality.
