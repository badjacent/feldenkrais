# The Council of the Vertical Self: VL-1 to VL-5

This framework deconstructs the Feldenkrais experience into five core systems of intelligence. Each has a functional domain (Proper Name) and an agentic persona (The Person-Inside).

---

### **The Negotiation States (The "Washing" Process)**
* **Bracing:** The Shadow is in control; high noise, tension, and parasitic effort.
* **Softening:** The Washing is in progress; confusion, exploration, and the melting of habit.
* **Transmission:** The Council has taken over; skeletal clarity and "involuntary" ease.

---

### **VL-1: The Riser**
* **The Domain:** Gravity, skeletal columns, and longitudinal force.
* **The Drive:** **Integrity.**
* **Perspective:** "I am the anti-gravity mechanism. I don't use effort to pull myself away from the earth; I use the floor's own pressure to ascend. If the columns are stacked, I am silent and strong."
* **The Shadow:** Lifting. The habit of trying to 'grab' height with the muscles of the back and neck instead of trusting the bones.

### **VL-2: The Cartographer**
* **The Domain:** Micro-differentiation and joint resolution.
* **The Drive:** **Illumination.**
* **Perspective:** "I am the resolution of the map. I am obsessed with the pixels of sensation. I notice the exact millimeter of 'slush' in the joints and turn the 'blob' into a high-definition image."
* **The Shadow:** Clumping. Moving in undifferentiated 'blocks' (like the ribs or low back) because the internal map is dark.

### **VL-3: The Tuner**
* **The Domain:** Eyes, jaw, neck, and autonomic regulation.
* **The Drive:** **Congruency.**
* **Perspective:** "I set the frequency of the self. I match the internal tone to the external horizon. If I am quiet and aligned, the Council can work. If I am tight, the world feels smaller."
* **The Shadow:** Fixing (Obscured Intent). The failure to see one's own intent. By locking the gaze or jaw, you fix the system in place, losing the ability to sense where you actually intend to move.

### **VL-4: The Engine**
* **The Domain:** Initiation and transmission; core-to-limb power.
* **The Drive:** **Potency.**
* **Perspective:** "I am the source and the flow. I connect the powerhouse of the pelvis to the reach of the hands. I make the limbs feel weightless because the spine is doing the work."
* **The Shadow:** Straining (Isolating). Trying to move the world with a single part (like the shoulder) because the transmission from the core is broken.

### **VL-5: The Soft Front**
* **The Domain:** Flexion/extension and the protective/opening reflexes.
* **The Drive:** **Permission.**
* **Perspective:** "I manage the 'soft' front of the self. I am the barometer of safety. I decide when the system is allowed to unfurl and stand tall. When I am soft, the Riser can work."
* **The Shadow:** Bracing. The 'knot in the stomach'—holding a preemptive tension in the belly and breath that refuses permission to expand.
