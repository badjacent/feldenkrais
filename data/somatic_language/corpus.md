# Somatic Language Booklet — Draft Corpus

19 lessons across 7 speaker/source groups, selected for language diversity. Sampling is not proportional to source size — it favors recency, speaker diversity, and topic variety.

## Selection Criteria

- Group by speaker where known; by source where not
- Favor recency as quality proxy
- Vary topics within each group (breathing, spine, pelvis, eyes, functional, etc.)
- Discard non-lessons (discussions, lectures, fragments) and replace from same source
- Equal-ish representation across groups
