# Pass B: VL Framework Synthesis

## Overview
You are provided with:
1. **The original lesson text**
2. **Pass A analysis** containing:
   - VL Framework classification (scores and rationale)
   - SPIFFER-Q decomposed steps

Your task is to apply the specified VL Framework lens to interpret the lesson through the Negotiation Framework.

---

## The Negotiation Framework

For each step from Pass A, evaluate the **Negotiation State**:

### Domination
The student is "doing" the movement through voluntary muscular force.
- Localized effort
- Speed, urgency
- Bracing, holding breath
- Linear "trying"

### Negotiation
The student is "exploring" the riddle.
- Oscillation, rhythm ("Washing" markers)
- Micro-movements
- Healthy confusion
- Trial and error

### Integration
The subcortical system (The Foundation) has found the solution.
- "Ease," "lightness"
- Global involvement
- Skeletal transmission of force
- "Involuntary" quality

---

## Framework-Specific Lens

Apply the VL Framework logic provided below. For each step, evaluate:
- Evidence of **Striver Mode** vs **Foundation Mode**
- Progress toward the **Functional Goal**
- The specific **Question** being asked through this framework's lens

## The Person Inside

Each VL framework defines an inner experiencer — a "person inside the person" who receives the lesson from a specific point of view. The VL file describes this inner being and their shadow (the Striver in that VL's terms).

For each row of the analytical table, write:
- **`vl_voice`**: A 1-2 sentence first-person account from the Person Inside. What do they perceive, feel, or notice at this point in the lesson? Write as if this inner being is narrating their experience. Use present tense.
- **`striver_shadow`**: A 1-2 sentence account of what the Shadow/Striver is doing at this point. Are they gripping? Bracing? Beginning to loosen? Have they stood down?

The arc across the rows should show the Striver gradually disarming as the lesson progresses.

### 1. Negotiation Framework Observations
