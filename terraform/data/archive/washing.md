# Feldenkrais Lesson Analysis Protocol (VL-1, v2.0)

## Overview
Analyze the lesson transcript as a dialogue between the **Postural Effort System (The Shadow)** and the **Postural Support System (The Foundation)**. The goal is to identify how the lesson "washes" away cortical noise to reveal the **Vertical Self**—a state of skeletal efficiency and longitudinal transmission of force.

## Phase 1: SPIFFER-Q Tagging
Deconstruct the text into discrete steps using these tags:
* **S (Sensation):** Sensory feedback; mapping pressures/contours.
* **P (Pauses):** Integration periods; "resting as you are."
* **I (Imagery):** Analogies; metaphors; mental geometric guides.
* **F (Function):** The global goal (e.g., walking, reaching, uprightness).
* **F (Focus):** Directing the internal spotlight to a specific anatomical point.
* **E (Effort):** Instructions for reduction of tone or parasitic work.
* **R (Relationships):** Connection between disparate parts (e.g., head to pelvis).
* **Q (The Question):** The neurological riddle (e.g., "Can you move the ribs without the neck joining?").

## Phase 2: The Negotiation Framework
Evaluate the interaction between the two internal intelligences:
1.  **The Shadow (Effort):** Cortical, voluntary, goal-oriented. Its failure mode is "noise" (bracing).
2.  **The Foundation (Support):** Subcortical, reflexive, seeks equilibrium. Its aspiration is "Quiet."
3.  **The Washing Gradient:** The gradual erosion of the Shadow through repetition, oscillation, or complexity.

---

## Phase 3: Structured Query Output

### 1. The Somatic Narrative (Linear Chunks)
* **The Primary Inquiry:** Summarize the fundamental neurological riddle of the lesson.
* **The Washing Gradient:** Describe how the Shadow is gradually eroded (e.g., through speed, complexity, or unusual configurations).
* **The Threshold of Quiet:** Pinpoint the moment where the Foundation takes over and the Vertical Self emerges.

### How to use the "Negotiation State" column (column4):
* **Bracing:** The Shadow is in control (high noise, parasitic effort).
* **Softening:** The Washing is in progress (confusion, exploration, melting of habit).
* **Transmission:** The Council has taken over (skeletal clarity, "involuntary" ease).
