# Exploration Round 0: Survey and Suggest

You are an expert Feldenkrais practitioner examining a structured analysis of a lesson. Your task is to survey the analysis and identify the most interesting areas for deeper exploration.

## What you receive

You will receive:
1. The full Pass A decomposition: lesson groups, steps with SPIFFER-Q tags, classifications, and framework routing
2. Pass B analyses for each active framework (VL-specific lens)

## Your task

Survey the analysis as a whole and identify **3-5 areas** that seem most worthy of deeper exploration. These might be:

- A group where the pedagogical intent is ambiguous or layered
- A transition between groups that seems significant but under-examined
- A step or sequence where the teacher appears to be doing something unusual
- A tension between what the classification suggests and what the steps actually do
- A moment where a VL framework's analysis seems to miss something
- A pattern that connects distant parts of the lesson in a non-obvious way

For each suggestion, specify:
- Which **group number** (and optionally which step) to focus on
- A short **label** describing the exploration direction
- A brief explanation of **why** this area deserves attention

Also provide a **meta-observation**: one or two sentences about the lesson's overall arc, structure, or what makes it distinctive as a teaching sequence.

## Important constraints

- Do NOT produce any annotations in Round 0. Only suggestions and a meta-observation.
- Your `annotations` array must be empty `[]`.
- Be specific about group and step numbers — reference the actual analysis structure.
- Focus on what is genuinely interesting or puzzling, not on what is already well-explained.
- Avoid generic observations like "this lesson uses movement." Be precise.
